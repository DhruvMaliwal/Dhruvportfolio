The context

01 / 04

Since June 2026 I have been doing my PS-II internship at Tell M (Curious Digital Pvt. Ltd.) in Bengaluru, as Operations Intern and Program Manager. I own a cluster of 80+ households and everything that keeps their service running: inventory tracking, meal plan generation and end-to-end ordering.

Most of that work used to be manual. A cook photographs the kitchen, someone reads the photo and types the numbers in, someone else writes the week's meal plan, and someone else again reconciles orders and invoices. I rebuilt each step as a small automation on top of the team's internal Copilot. Together they cut the full loop from 3 hours to 13 minutes per household.

The plan writes itself, then briefs the cook.

Copilot-based meal plans generated for each household, followed by an automated briefing for the cook so the plan actually reaches the kitchen without a person relaying it.

BeforeSomeone writes the week's meal plan and passes it along.

Every receipt lands in one place and invoices itself.

A cross-platform tracker that collects ordering receipts in one place and generates the invoices automatically through the internal Copilot, closing the ordering loop end to end.

How I work

04 / 04

Start from the loop, not the tool

The useful question is where the hours go between the cook's photo and the invoice. The tool follows from that answer.

02

Ship the smallest version

Every tool here is a thin automation on the team's internal Copilot, not a new platform. Small enough to ship, big enough to remove a manual step.
